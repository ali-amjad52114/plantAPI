What actually happened

They found it. Five weeks later, it exploded.

“The problem was found. The replacement was planned. It just never got scheduled in time.”

A worker found a leak in a gas valve on July 8. The cracked valve was patched, and the company planned to take the section offline and replace it. The valve was made in 1953 and refurbished in 2013. Thirty-four days later, before the replacement happened, it exploded. The safety board called it an informal procedure and a weak safety system — and said it should never have happened. The problem was found. The replacement was planned. It just never got scheduled in time.
